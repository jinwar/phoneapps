How it works

Your phone’s motion sensor measures the bounce of each jump. Counts are estimates, not verified jumps. Keep this app visible and the screen unlocked; switching apps pauses counting. The app requests a screen wake lock when supported.

Workouts stay on this device. No account or Samsung Health connection is needed. Install from your browser’s menu for quick access; after the first visit, the app works offline.
