Teach the counter

After recording, choose the actual type and count. Optional start/end times exclude putting the phone in your pocket and taking it out. The activity label is only for calibration, not required to start. Labels apply only to that time range. Use one type per calibration recording.

Forward/backward suburi (zenshin-kōtai suburi) uses forward and backward footwork. Hayasuburi is fast suburi with repeated hopping forward and back.
